# CS-250_Portfolio

When interpreting user needs, it's important to break down the needs to their most basic functions. You have to make sure that those needs are able to be implemented into the program. This responsibility is given to the Product Owner. They are able to interpret the most important needs to the developers and help the developers know what is needed within the program. That's why "user stories" are important because when trying to present such an idea to the team, you have to give them an understanding of why this new function needs to be implemented and make the team care about change or implementation.

My typical approach when developing programs, I write in a journal what the program needs to do and break down the functions in my journal what needs to happen to make this program work. I hope to incorporate some Agile processes into my future development work. For example, prioritizing my backlog with the important functions that actually work. I know that I can get carried away, before the program is actually "done", and try to add features and fun stuff before I even have a working app. Having a functional app and then adding on to that is probably the best way to go about that.

To be a good team member, it is to be as thorough and transparent with your team as possible. Honesty will allow the team and the project moving forward. If you're not honest about what is going on with your section that you're responsible for, the project can fall behind and the team won't be able to pinpoint the problem immediately. It can waste time and make the team suffer. To be a good team member is to be a team player.
